What Is HotWax Commerce?

HotWax Commerce is an omnichannel order and inventory management platform built for retailers using Shopify and Shopify POS.

It connects ecommerce, stores, warehouses, fulfillment partners, and ERP systems so inventory can be promised accurately and orders can be routed, fulfilled, and reconciled across the retail network.

Shopify remains the commerce and point-of-sale experience. NetSuite, Acumatica, or another ERP can remain the financial system of record. HotWax coordinates the inventory, order, and fulfillment decisions between them.

Own the work that fits your business

You do not need to cover every role. HotWax has teams for product configuration, solution design, integrations, project coordination, QA, training, launch, and support. Choose where your team adds value and HotWax will cover the rest.

What qualifies

Accepted introduction: A relevant stakeholder understands the purpose and accepts a substantive meeting.

Qualified opportunity: HotWax confirms a real operating problem, a credible stakeholder, a reason to act, and an agreed next step.

Closed-won customer: The retailer signs an agreement and HotWax collects eligible subscription revenue.

{% module_block module "partner_program_named_bounties" path="@hubspot/rich_text", label="Named account bounties" %} {% module_attribute "html" %}

Named-account bounties

Approved partners may receive a confidential list of retailers HotWax wants to reach. Each available account can include its tier, fit rationale, desired stakeholder, rewards, claim expiration, and account-specific conditions.

The target list is available only after the required NDA and partner terms are complete. It may not be forwarded or redistributed.

{% module_block module "partner_program_claim_rules" path="@hubspot/rich_text", label="Claim and payment rules" %} {% module_attribute "html" %}

Claim and payment rules

  • Register the account before the introduction or within seven days afterward.
  • A new claim normally remains active for 90 days while the partner secures an accepted introduction.
  • After an accepted introduction, attribution normally remains protected for 12 months while the opportunity remains active.
  • An account already in an active HotWax sales process is not automatically eligible.
  • Introduction and qualification rewards are normally paid within 15 business days after approval and required documentation.
  • Closed-won percentages are paid only on eligible customer revenue collected by HotWax.

Final offers remain subject to the signed partner agreement, account-specific written approval, and HotWax confirmation of attribution and milestone completion.
