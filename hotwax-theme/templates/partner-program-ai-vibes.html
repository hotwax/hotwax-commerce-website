What is HotWax Commerce?

The order and inventory layer for complex Shopify retail.

HotWax Commerce connects ecommerce, stores, warehouses, fulfillment partners, and ERP systems so retailers can promise inventory accurately and route every order to the right location.

Shopify remains the commerce experience. NetSuite, Acumatica, or another ERP can remain the enterprise system of record. HotWax coordinates the operational decisions between them.
